Mert Personal Google

Mert Personal Google is a private, single-user application. It connects its owner's own Google account (Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive) to the owner's personal assistant software, which runs on a computer the owner controls.

It is not a product or a service. It is not offered to the public, there is nothing to sign up for, and nobody other than the owner can use it.

With the owner's permission, the application reads the owner's email, calendar and files, prepares draft emails, and keeps the owner's calendar and documents organized.
