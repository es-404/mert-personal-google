Privacy policy

Mert Personal Google · Effective November 12, 2026

Who this applies to

Mert Personal Google is a private application with one user: its owner. It accesses only Google accounts that belong to the owner and that the owner has signed in and approved. It does not collect data from anyone else, and there are no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent through Google's sign-in screen, the application can read the owner's Gmail messages, create and update email drafts, read and add events on the owner's Google Calendar, and read and organize files in the owner's Google Drive.

How the data is used

The data is used only to carry out the owner's own requests: summarizing and searching email, preparing drafts, checking availability, and keeping documents organized. To do that, content may be processed by the AI services the owner has chosen to run the assistant.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in credentials and any saved copies of the owner's data are kept on computers and accounts the owner controls. The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone other than the processing described above.

Removing access

The owner can remove the application's access at any time from the Google Account page under Security, "Your connections to third-party apps and services".

Contact

Questions can be raised through the issues page of this site's repository.
